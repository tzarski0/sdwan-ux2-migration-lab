Step 1: Verify Current Network State¶

Before changing anything, confirm the network is healthy and record how the current UX 1.0 policy identifies sites.

  • Go to Monitor → Devices and verify that all devices have full control and data connections.

  • Go to Configuration → Devices → WAN Edges and confirm that all routers are In Sync and managed by device templates.

  • Go to Configuration → Classic → Policies and verify that the centralized policy is activated.

  • On the same screen, click … next to the policy, then click Preview. Note the site lists the policy uses:

    Site list Site IDs
    dc 10
    branches 20-99
